Privacy policy

Plain summary. To make a report we need two things, the street address you want screened and an email address to send it to. We send the street address to public data services and a mapping service to build the report. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising. Payment details go to our payment processor, not to us.

Draft for counsel review, not yet in force

1. Who this policy covers

This policy describes how Groundsheet, a product of Ashcroft and Thorn [legal entity name] ("Groundsheet," "we," "us") handles information when you visit getgroundsheet.com or groundsheet.report (the "Site") or order a Groundsheet environmental screening report (the "Report").

2. What we collect

We collect only what the order requires:

  • The street address to be screened. This is the subject of the Report. It is an address of a place, and we treat it as such; it need not be your home or a property you own.
  • Your email address. Used to deliver the Report, send the receipt and answer support requests.
  • Order details. The order number, the time of the order, the price paid and the delivery status, so that we can find your order if you write to us.
  • For members (agents, inspectors, offices): the name, licence number and brokerage or company you ask us to print on the co-branded cover, and the billing details the membership needs.

We do not ask a buyer for a name, phone number, physical address or any other personal information. Our payment processor, [payment processor; Stripe when live], collects your payment details directly under its own privacy policy; we receive confirmation of payment and the last four digits of the card, and we never see or store full card numbers.

If you email hello@getgroundsheet.com, we keep the correspondence so that we can respond and keep a record of what was resolved.

3. How we use it

  • To generate the Report for the address you supplied.
  • To deliver the Report and the receipt to your email address.
  • To process refunds, credits and corrections under the Refund Policy and the Terms.
  • To answer your support requests.
  • To keep records required for tax and accounting.
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse of the Site.

We do not use your email address for marketing unless you separately opt in to receive updates from Groundsheet, and you can withdraw that at any time.

4. Where the address goes

Building a Report means asking public services about the address. When you order, the street address (and the latitude and longitude we derive from it) is sent to:

  • the US Census Bureau geocoder, to locate the address;
  • federal data services operated by the EPA, FEMA, USGS, USFWS, USFS, EIA, NPS, NOAA, FAA and the Census Bureau, and state services operated by state geological surveys, air agencies and natural-resource departments, to retrieve records within the screening radii;
  • [mapping and imagery provider], to render the maps that appear in the Report; and
  • [parcel data provider, Regrid, when parcel lines are added], to retrieve the parcel boundary.

These services receive the location and nothing else about you. We do not send your email address or order details to any of them. Each service handles requests under its own terms, which we do not control.

When a Report is reviewed by an automated system for quality (for example, a check that every figure matches its source), the address and the Report's content are processed under terms that do not permit the provider to train on them. [counsel: question list item 30]

5. Who else receives information

  • Payment processor ([payment processor]): receives your payment details to charge the order.
  • Email delivery provider ([email provider]): receives your email address in order to deliver the Report and receipt.
  • Hosting provider ([hosting provider; Cloudflare Pages for the Site]): serves the Site and stores order records and generated Reports.
  • Member agents and inspectors: if a member ordered the Report for you, the member receives the Report and knows the address; the member does not receive your payment details from us.
  • Legal and compliance. We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.
  • Business transfer. If Groundsheet is acquired or merges, order records may transfer to the successor, who will be bound by this policy.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information, and we have not sold it in the preceding twelve months.
  • We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • We do not build profiles of you, and we do not combine your order with data from other sources.
  • We do not use the addresses you screen to market to the occupants of those addresses.
  • We do not publish the score or findings of any address except in the Report ordered for it. The two sample reports on the Site are public by design and the addresses in them were chosen for that purpose.

7. Retention

Order records (email, address screened, order number, amount, dates) are kept for [retention period] to satisfy tax, accounting and refund obligations, then deleted. Generated Reports are kept for [report retention period] so that you can request a re-send, then deleted. Support correspondence is kept for [support retention period]. You may ask us to delete your order record sooner; see section 9.

8. Cookies and analytics

The Site sets no cookies of its own. [analytics statement: either "We use no analytics." or "We use a cookieless analytics provider that does not collect IP addresses, to count page views."] Our payment processor may set cookies on its checkout page under its own policy. The Site is served from a content delivery network that keeps standard server logs for a short period for security.

9. Your choices and rights

You may, at any time, email hello@getgroundsheet.com to:

  • ask what information we hold about your order;
  • ask us to correct your email address;
  • ask us to delete your order record and any stored Reports, subject to records we are required to keep for tax and accounting;
  • withdraw any marketing consent you gave.

We will respond within [response window, e.g. 30 days]. We will need to confirm the request came from the email address on the order.

10. California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, gives you the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use and disclose it; to request deletion; to request correction; to opt out of sale or sharing (we do neither); to limit the use of sensitive personal information (we collect none); and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Categories of personal information collected in the preceding twelve months: identifiers (email address; for members, name and licence number) and commercial information (order records). Source: you. Purpose: fulfilling your order, as described in section 3. Disclosed for a business purpose to: the service providers listed in section 5. Sold or shared: none.

To exercise your rights, email hello@getgroundsheet.com with "California privacy request" in the subject line, or write to [mailing address]. You may designate an authorised agent to make a request on your behalf; we will ask the agent for proof of authorisation. 11. Other US state privacy laws

Residents of [other states with comprehensive privacy laws; counsel to confirm the list and applicability thresholds] may have similar rights of access, correction, deletion and portability, and the right to appeal a refused request. The contact in section 15 handles all such requests.

12. Security

Order records and Reports are stored with [hosting provider] using access controls and encryption in transit. Payment details never reach our systems. No method of storage is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your information, we will notify you as required by law.

13. Children

The Site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

14. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is posted at getgroundsheet.com/privacy/ with its effective date. Material changes will be noted at the top of the page for at least 30 days.
