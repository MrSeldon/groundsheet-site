What happens next

  1. Minutes, not days. The engine queries 24 public federal and state services around the confirmed point, draws every map, scores sixteen sections and reconciles each figure to its record. Most reports finish in under ten minutes; the longest we have seen is about twenty when a federal service is slow.
  2. Two things in your inbox. The PDF, about 40 pages, and a private link to the web version with the same findings. Forward either to your agent, your inspector, your lender or your attorney; the licence covers the people in your transaction.
  3. If a source fails. The affected section reads "Not screened", the cover reads "Checks: flagged", and a credit for a rerun arrives without you asking. If we cannot generate the report at all, you are refunded in full.
  4. If the point was wrong. Write to us within fourteen days and we run the corrected address at no charge or refund the order, your choice.

Before you order

The report is a screening of public records for one point. It is not a Phase I environmental site assessment, not a home inspection, not a California Natural Hazard Disclosure Statement, not an insurance, engineering or legal opinion, and it measures nothing at the house. Read a sample first if you have not; both are public in full.
