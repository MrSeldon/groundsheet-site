Write to us; a person reads it

One mailbox for orders, agents, inspectors, press, corrections and counsel. We answer within a business day during the founding period.

Email

hello@getgroundsheet.com

Put one of these in the subject line and it reaches the right place:

  • Order: an address and the email the report should go to. Until checkout opens we run it by hand and invoice with the report.
  • Founding agent: your name, brokerage and the next address you have under contract. Ten seats in Santa Cruz County through 31 January 2027; the ask is written out here.
  • Inspector pilot: your company, your software, and roughly how many inspections a month.
  • Office or volume: for brokerages, franchises and associations; we will reply with the ladder and a real report on a house in your market.
  • Correction: a report id (it begins PLM) and the page. A wrong line gets fixed and the report rerun at no charge. This is also the dispute channel named in the draft terms.
  • Privacy: a request under the privacy policy, from the email on the order.
  • Press: we will send the two sample PDFs and answer questions in writing.

Post

Groundsheet, a product of Ashcroft and Thorn. A mailing address is added here when the entity and the registered address are confirmed with counsel, before launch.

What we do not do by email

We do not give advice about a particular house beyond what its report says, and we do not say whether you should buy it. The report tells you what the public record holds and which specialist answers the next question; the specialist is the person to ask.
