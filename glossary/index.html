Glossary · the codes key

Every code the report prints, defined in one line: 174 entries under 13 headings

The report never prints a code it does not explain. This is the key at the back of every Groundsheet, on the web, for both launch states so far, with an anchor per code.

Every code, acronym and threshold the Groundsheet report prints, one line each, under the same 13 headings the report's codes key uses. In the report a small raised k after a code (RCRAk) means the code is defined in the key at the back; the section page it appears on also keys it in its own "Reading this section" annex. This page is that key for both launch states so far: 3 rows are California only and 3 are Florida only, marked beside the line; the other rows print in every report. A section page or a report viewer links a code here as /glossary/#RCRA.

Regulatory programmes 25 · Facility tiers and designations 10 · Flood and terrain 11 · Wildfire 8 · Geology and seismic 22 · Radon 9 · Species 11 · Wetlands 22 · Pipelines 5 · Community 13 · Air quality 16 · Wind and storms 7 · Map and data terms 15

The codes key is built by the report engine, not written by hand for the site: this page runs the same function the PDF uses on the two sample reports (Aptos, California and Tampa, Florida) and prints the union, 174 rows on 2026-10-14. When a report prints a code that is not in its key, the engine's build fails, so the key cannot drift from the report.

Regulatory programmes 25

AAI
All Appropriate Inquiries, 40 CFR Part 312: the standard a Phase I ESA must meet.
ASTM E1527-21
Standard practice for Phase I Environmental Site Assessments.also E1527-21, E1527, ASTM
CAA
Clean Air Act; a CAA registration alone makes a Tier III facility.
CERCLA
Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act: Superfund.
CFR
Code of Federal Regulations, cited as title and part (40 CFR 312).
CWA
Clean Water Act: discharge permits and the Section 404 wetlands permit.
CZMA
Coastal Zone Management Act: state review of development in the coastal zone.
ECHO
EPA Enforcement and Compliance History Online, the regulated-facility record.
EPA
US Environmental Protection Agency.
ESA
Endangered Species Act.
FR
Federal Register, cited as volume and page (78 FR 8746); where listings are published.
NHPA
National Historic Preservation Act.
NPL
National Priorities List: the Superfund list of the most serious contaminated sites.
Phase I ESA
Phase I Environmental Site Assessment (ASTM E1527-21); this report is not one.
RCRA
Resource Conservation and Recovery Act, the federal hazardous-waste programme; each handler carries one designation (LQG, SQG, VSQG or TSD), set by the waste it generates in a month or whether it treats others' waste.
LQG
Large quantity generator: 1,000 kg (2,200 lb) or more a month, or over 1 kg acutely hazardous; strictest generator rules; Tier II. Printed RCRA-LQG.
SQG
Small quantity generator: 100 to 1,000 kg a month; Tier II. Printed RCRA-SQG.
VSQG
Very small quantity generator: under 100 kg a month (formerly CESQG); Tier III. Printed RCRA-VSQG.
TSD
Treatment, storage or disposal facility: permitted to take others' hazardous waste; Tier I. Printed RCRA-TSD.
RCRAInfo
EPA's RCRA handler database, the source of each designation.
REC
Recognized Environmental Condition: the Phase I ESA finding of a likely release.
RY2024
TRI reporting year 2024, the latest release filings available at generation.
SDWA
Safe Drinking Water Act.
TRI
Toxics Release Inventory: annual chemical-release reporting by larger facilities.
VCP
Voluntary cleanup programme: a state scheme for owner-led cleanup to agreed standards.

Geology and seismic 22

ASCE 7
The structural load standard that defines the seismic design values printed here.also ASCE
B1, B2, B3
USGS karst exposure classes: B1 carbonate buried under less than 50 ft of cover, B2 under 50 to 200 ft, B3 under more than 200 ft; E is exposed at the surface.also B1, B2, B3
CGS
California Geological Survey, publisher of the state geologic map.also CGS Geologic Map of California
Cover-collapse
A sinkhole that opens abruptly when the soil cover falls into a cavity in the rock below; the damaging kind.
Cover-subsidence
A sinkhole that forms slowly as sand cover settles into the rock; gradual, rarely sudden.
FGS
Florida Geological Survey, keeper of the state's subsidence incident reports (sinkhole reports filed by the public and insurers).
GPM
Gallons per minute, the well yield unit.
HydroShare
The CUAHSI (university water-science consortium) data repository that publishes USGWD.also CUAHSI
Karst
Terrain on soluble rock (limestone, dolomite, gypsum) where water has opened cavities; sinkholes, springs and fast groundwater movement follow.
Ma
Million years ago, the age unit on the geologic column.
OFR 2014-1156
USGS Open-File Report 2014-1156, the national karst map compilation (Weary and Doctor 2014) that draws the carbonate and pseudokarst polygons.also OFR
PGA
Peak ground acceleration, in g (share of gravity).
S1
Mapped spectral acceleration at a 1-second period on site class BC, in g.
SD1
Design spectral acceleration at 1 second (ASCE 7), in g; governs taller buildings.
SDC
Seismic Design Category, ASCE 7: A (lowest) to E/F, from design accelerations and soil.
SDS
Design spectral acceleration at short periods (ASCE 7), in g; governs low, stiff buildings.
SIR
Subsidence incident report: one filed sinkhole or ground-collapse event in the FGS register, with its date, size and rate as reported.
Site class BC
ASCE 7 default ground class, soft rock to very dense soil (VS30 about 760 m/s), assumed without a soil survey.also BC
SS
Mapped spectral acceleration at short period (0.2 s) on site class BC, in g.
USGS
US Geological Survey.
USGWD
United States Groundwater Well Database, 14.2 million well records.
VS30
Average shear-wave velocity in the top 30 m of ground, in m/s; sets the site class.

Radon 9

4 pCi/L
EPA action level: fix a home at or above it; consider fixing from 2 pCi/L.
CBF
Census cartographic boundary file, the 1:500,000 county outlines on the radon map.
eU
Equivalent uranium, in ppm: the NURE measure from gamma radiation.
NRPP
National Radon Proficiency Program, a certifier of radon testers and mitigators.
NRSB
National Radon Safety Board, the other national certifier.
NURE
USGS National Uranium Resource Evaluation, the 1970s airborne survey behind the surface-uranium map.
pCi/L
Picocuries per litre of air, the US radon unit; about 37 becquerels per cubic metre.
Radon zones
EPA county zones: 1 predicted above 4 pCi/L indoors, 2 between 2 and 4, 3 below 2.also Zones 1 · 2 · 3
Rn-222
Radon-222, the gas from uranium-238 decaying via radium in soil and rock.

E / T
Status beside each name: E Endangered, T Threatened under the ESA.also E/T
ECOS
USFWS Environmental Conservation Online System, the listed-species record and county list.
ECOS code
ECOS species identifier, a letter and three characters (E071); the key to its page at ecos.fws.gov.

IPaC
USFWS Information for Planning and Consultation, the online species and habitat query tool.
Section 7
ESA consultation: federal agencies consult USFWS so their actions do not jeopardise a species.
Section 9
ESA prohibition on take of listed animals anywhere; plants on federal land.
Take
Harass, harm, hunt, wound, kill, trap or collect; harm includes habitat change that injures.
USFWS
US Fish and Wildlife Service.

R

P

UB

SB

FO
Class: forested, trees over 6 m tall.
SS
Class: scrub-shrub, woody plants under 6 m.
EM

Class: aquatic bed, plants on or below the water surface.
US

Wind and storms 7

EF scale
Enhanced Fujita tornado damage scale: EF0 65 to 85 mph, EF1 86 to 110, EF2 111 to 135, EF3 136 to 165, EF4 166 to 200, EF5 over 200; F0 to F5 is the older Fujita scale, used before February 2007.also EF
HURDAT2
NOAA National Hurricane Center best-track database: each Atlantic (1851 on) and northeast Pacific (1949 on) cyclone's centre, wind and status at six-hour steps.
NHC
National Hurricane Center, the NOAA office that forecasts tropical cyclones and keeps HURDAT2.
NRI
FEMA National Risk Index: a census-tract rating for 18 natural hazards (Very Low to Very High) from expected annual loss, social vulnerability and community resilience.
Saffir-Simpson
Hurricane categories by one-minute sustained wind: 1 is 74 to 95 mph, 2 is 96 to 110, 3 is 111 to 129, 4 is 130 to 156, 5 is 157 or more.
SPC
NOAA Storm Prediction Center; its tornado database runs from 1950 with a start and an end point for each tornado.
OIR-B1-1802
Florida's uniform wind mitigation inspection form (Office of Insurance Regulation), valid five years; each feature it records earns a premium discount under s. 627.0629.Florida onlyalso OIR, B1-1802

How to read a code in the report

Codes are set in the report's monospace face. The first time a code appears on a page it carries the raised k; later uses on the same page do not. Sub-rows (the RCRA generator tiers under RCRA, the Cowardin letters under NWI) read as one block under their parent. State rows appear only in reports for that state: a California report keys CAL FIRE and FRAP, a Florida report keys FGS and the sinkhole statute. Where a code is printed by a data source rather than by Groundsheet (a weather station's ICAO id, an NWI letter), the engine adds the row when it builds that report, so two reports can carry slightly different keys.
