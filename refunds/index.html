Refund policy

Plain summary. During the founding period you can get a full refund within 14 days of purchase for any reason. Separately, if your report's cover reads "Checks: flagged," you receive a credit for a future report automatically, without asking. Reports are built from public databases that sometimes lag or go offline; when that happens the report says so on its cover and method page, and this policy is how we make it right.

Draft for counsel review, not yet in force

1. Founding-period refund: 14 days, any reason

While the founding period is open (the first [N] Reports delivered, or until [founding period end date], whichever comes first), any Report purchased is eligible for a full refund of the price paid if you request it within 14 days of the purchase date. You do not need to give a reason. You may keep the Report.

We offer this because Groundsheet is new, and we would rather learn why a Report did not serve you than argue about it. If you are willing, tell us what was missing; it helps, and it is optional.

2. Automatic credit for a flagged report

Every Report prints a checks status on its cover. "Checks: passed" means every core source returned, every figure reconciled to its record and every cross-check passed. "Checks: flagged" means at least one source did not return or at least one check did not pass; the affected sections are marked "Not screened" and listed on the method page.

If the checks status of your Report is anything other than passed, we issue a credit for one additional Report to the email address on the order, automatically, within [credit issuance window, e.g. 24 hours] of delivery. You do not need to request it. The credit can be used for any address, including re-running the same address once the missing source is back, and it does not expire for [credit validity period, e.g. 12 months].

A flagged Report is still a complete document for the sources that did return. If you would prefer a refund instead of a credit, section 1 applies during the founding period, and section 6 applies afterward.

3. Reports that cannot be generated

If we cannot generate a Report at all for the address you supplied (for example, the address cannot be geocoded, or a core service is down for an extended period), we will tell you by email and refund the order in full. No request is needed.

4. Wrong address

The Site shows you the geocoded point before the Report runs, and the cover of each Report states the address as geocoded and how it was matched. If you entered an address incorrectly and a Report was generated for the wrong place, email us within 14 days and we will either run the corrected address at no charge or refund the order, your choice. If the geocoder matched your address to a different location than you intended and the cover shows a non-exact match, the same applies, with no 14-day limit.

5. Corrections

Draft for counsel. If a Report contains an error in transcribing a public record, the correction channel in section 15 of the Terms applies: we rerun the Report with the correction at no charge, explain in writing why it stands, or refund the order, within [10 business days]. A correction request does not shorten any refund window in this policy.

6. What a refund does not cover

Refunds are not available on the grounds that a Report's findings were unwelcome, that a section screened "Low" when you expected otherwise (or the reverse), or that the Report did not include information that lives only in private records or in records that have not yet reached the public databases it reads. The Report is screening-level, draws on public data of record, and inherits the gaps and lag in those sources. Those limits are stated on the Site before purchase, in the Terms of Service, and in the Report itself. Where a known gap affects your Report, section 2 applies.

7. Memberships

A membership is billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month and no further charges are made. Membership fees already billed are not refunded, except where we failed to provide the membership's features during that month, in which case we refund that month on request. Reports ordered under a membership follow sections 1 to 6 of this policy at the price paid for the Report.

8. After the founding period

After the founding period closes, sections 2 to 7 continue to apply in full. The 14-day any-reason refund in section 1 is replaced by: [founder to decide: keep 14-day any-reason; shorten to 7 days; or move to credit-only with refund on request for cause.] This page will state the applicable rule and its effective date.

9. How to request a refund

Email hello@getgroundsheet.com from the email address used on the order, with the order number from your receipt. If you do not have the order number, the address screened and the approximate purchase date are enough. We acknowledge requests within [acknowledgement window, e.g. 2 business days] and process approved refunds within [processing window, e.g. 5 business days].

10. How refunds and credits are paid

Refunds are returned to the original payment method through [payment processor]. The processor's own posting times apply, usually 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank. Credits are issued as a single-use code tied to the email address on the order and are redeemed at checkout. Credits have no cash value and cannot be transferred or combined with other offers unless we say otherwise.
