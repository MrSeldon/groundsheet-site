The volume ladder

The data and compute behind a report cost under three dollars. What scales is support, so the price follows volume on one account, per calendar month:

Reports a month on one account Price per report Who this usually is
1 $89 a buyer, once
2 to 9 $49, with the $39 membership an agent
10 to 99 $39 a busy agent or a small office
100 to 999 $29 a brokerage, a franchise region, an inspector network
1,000 and up $19 to $20 an association, a franchise head office, a disclosure channel

The tier applies to the whole month once the count is reached; nobody pays $49 for the ninth report and $39 for the tenth.

The membership, explained

The membership is $39 a month and it is low on purpose: it buys the habit, not the margin. What it includes, in the words we use inside:

  • Co-branded cover. The cover carries "Presented by [your name, licence, brokerage]" and a line below it, "Research and report by Groundsheet". The method, the sources and the authorship stay ours; the client sees you brought it.
  • The leave-behind cut. A two-page version, the cover and the summary page with the follow-up panel, for the showing, the open house, the kitchen table. The full 40 pages sit behind it as the PDF.
  • Unlimited rerun on the same address during escrow. The data moves while a deal is open: a new fire perimeter, a new enforcement action, a FEMA geometry that failed on Tuesday and returns on Wednesday, a corrected address. A member regenerates the same address as often as they like for 60 days at no charge. A non-member buys a report once.
  • Cancel any month. No term, no minimum. Reports already delivered stay yours.

Agents buy the report for the client. Groundsheet pays no referral fees to agents, in any state, and the membership is a flat price rather than a discount tied to anything you refer. The reasons are legal (RESPA section 8 and California Business and Professions Code 10148 are being reviewed by counsel) and simpler than legal: the agent who pays for the report is the agent who reads it.

The inspector line

Home inspectors sell the report as an add-on beside the inspection. During the pilot the wholesale price is $39 a report with a suggested retail of $89, the inspector keeping the difference. At volume the wholesale price follows the ladder above: $29 from a hundred a month, $20 from a thousand, which is where a franchise head office lands. Delivery goes with the inspection report; the Spectora path is described on the inspectors page.

The founding agents

Ten buyer's agents in Santa Cruz County, taken in the order they say yes, through 31 January 2027 or until the ten seats are filled, whichever comes first. Each gets the membership free for six months and free reports on up to three live transactions. In return we ask for an hour of honest feedback after each report and, if the report earned it, a sentence we can print with your name. There is no obligation to recommend it and no referral arrangement; the seat is paid for in feedback. The ask is written out on the agents page.

Payment, refunds, taxes

Payment is by card at checkout, which opens when Stripe goes live; until then orders are taken by hand through the contact page. Prices are in US dollars and exclude sales tax where it applies. The refund policy is short: fourteen days for any reason during the founding period, a credit for any report whose checks line reads flagged, a full refund if we cannot generate a report for the address at all.
