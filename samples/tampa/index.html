The Tampa report is the dense fixture: hundreds of permitted facilities, a chemical registry, and two inserts the Aptos house never triggers. It has no listed-species section because USFWS designates no critical habitat near enough, and no pipeline section because the nearest EIA gas transmission segment is 5.35 km away, outside the 5 km radius the section uses; a section that has nothing to say is left out rather than padded.

What the ground says here, in the report's own order: seven Tier II facilities a mile east and nothing in Tier I; outside the floodplain; a moderate wildfire class with eight fires in thirty miles; limestone under the lot with thirty sinkhole reports in five miles and none in one; very low shaking; a county radon prediction of 2 to 4 pCi/L; the bay's estuarine water within half a mile; eleven schools and four clinics within a mile; a general-aviation airport whose firefighting-foam screening radius reaches the lot, a potential source and not a measured release; good air on 278 days of 2025; and six hurricanes within 50 mi since 1950, Milton the strongest.

The PDF is the 14 October 2026 render. It still carries the working name Prelim on its cover.
