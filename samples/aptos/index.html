The Aptos report is the founder's defect fixture: the house the design was argued over, page by page, through three sittings. It is the report most people should read first because it shows two High sections, seismic hazard and listed species, inside a Moderate composite, which is exactly the case where a single word on a cover would mislead and the follow-up panel earns its place.

What the ground says here, in the report's own order: no Tier I or II facility within two miles; outside the floodplain; a short but large fire record on the Santa Cruz Mountains side; alluvial fan deposits over a high seismic design category; a county radon prediction of 2 to 4 pCi/L; critical habitat for the tidewater goby a few blocks away and 34 other listed species in the county; four wetlands within half a mile and none on the lot; a gas transmission line half a mile west; two schools within a mile; good air almost every day of 2025; and the six California statutory zones, all clear, the nearest a hair away.

The PDF is the 14 October 2026 render, after the QA pass described on the methodology page. It still carries the working name Prelim on its cover.
